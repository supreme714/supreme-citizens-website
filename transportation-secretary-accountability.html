Secretary's Public Duties and Television Production: A Documented Timeline

A source-linked review of transportation incidents during the reported production window of The Great American Road Trip, featuring Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy and his family.

Editorial verification: November 15, 2026. This is a historical research record, not a live incident feed.

← Transportation Spending Accountability Register

Evidence boundary: A transportation event occurring within the overall September 2025–April 2026 filming window does not establish that the Secretary was filming on that day, unavailable for official duties, or responsible for the event. Specific filming dates and official scheduling records have not been verified here.

What is confirmed about production?

Forbes reported eight separate short trips from September 2025 to April 2026. The series had corporate sponsors with business interests relevant to DOT oversight. In reporting quoted by Forbes, DOT said career ethics officials approved the Secretary's participation. In June 2026, six Democratic senators requested an inspector general investigation; the request itself is not a finding of misconduct.

Production window is reported in secondary journalism; individual filming dates have not been independently verified. The public timeline below uses original government incident records.

How the register evaluates evidence

  1. Incident fact: require an FAA, NTSB, DOT or equivalent dated source.
  2. Filming fact: distinguish reported production window from documented day-by-day itinerary.
  3. Conflict: do not mark an overlap without matching dated records; do not infer an operational failure from coincidence.
  4. Finding: distinguish congressional allegations, agency responses, ethics advice, inspector general findings and final adjudications.
  5. Updates: preserve source URLs, original dates and amendments. Label unresolved matters pending, not confirmed misconduct.
