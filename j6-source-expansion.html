PHASE 11 · DOCUMENTARY RECORD

SOURCE EXPANSION & RECORD POPULATION

The framework is built. Phase 11 expands the documentary record inside it.

New material should strengthen the public record without blurring the difference between a primary source, an allegation, testimony, a government finding, a judicial holding or a later development.

Open Phase 12 Documentary Record — J6-E-0011–0020 →

Population priorities

Build outward from the evidence

J6-E

Evidence Ledger

Add official and primary-source records with provenance, what each record establishes and explicit limitations.

J6-T

Timeline & Event Record

Add events only where chronology and institutional context can be supported by identified records.

J6-S

Security & Response

Keep warning, intelligence processing, requests, authorization, mobilization, deployment and response distinct.

J6-L

Legal Record

Track charges, pleas, verdicts, dismissals, appellate rulings, Supreme Court decisions and clemency as separate procedural stages.

J6-V

Voices & Testimony

Expand sworn testimony, direct observation, interviews, communications and conflicting accounts with corroboration notes.

Admission standard

What qualifies for the record

Prefer primary and official sources. Identify originator, date and provenance.
State the evidentiary boundary. Explain both what a source establishes and what it does not establish.
Preserve procedural distinctions. Allegations are not facts merely because they are repeated; charges are not convictions; congressional findings are not judicial findings.
Preserve history. Later rulings, reports and clemency should update the current record without silently erasing earlier procedural events.
Cross-reference rule: a connection between records shows relevance. It does not, by itself, prove causation, motive, guilt, innocence or legal responsibility.
