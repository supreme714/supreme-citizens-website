# Election Day Homepage Countdown

The homepage countdown targets the 2026 federal general Election Day: November 3, 2026.

Behavior:
- Before Election Day: displays days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining.
- On Election Day: changes to “ELECTION DAY IS TODAY — PARTICIPATE · VOTE.”
- Beginning November 4: changes to “CITIZENSHIP CONTINUES — AFTER ELECTION DAY.”
- The countdown banner links to `election-day.html`.
- The homepage includes a featured Election Day civic-holiday initiative section and Election Day navigation link.
- The design is responsive for mobile and desktop.

The countdown is calculated in the visitor's browser and does not require a daily deployment.
