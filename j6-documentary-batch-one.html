PHASE 12 · DOCUMENTARY RECORD EXPANSION

BATCH ONE: J6-E-0011–J6-E-0020

Ten additional official and primary-source records expand the documentary foundation while preserving the difference between testimony, government findings, allegations, verdicts and sentencing records.

J6-E-0011PRIMARY SOURCE

Select Committee Transcribed Interviews and Depositions Collection

Establishes: GovInfo preserves interviews, depositions, exhibits, court documents, hearing videos and other supporting materials released with the Select Committee record.

Boundary: A transcript establishes what a witness said; truth, completeness and weight may require corroboration.

View Official Source →
J6-E-0012GOVERNMENT FINDING

Senate Report — Examining the U.S. Capitol Attack

Establishes: The bipartisan Senate report documented intelligence, security-planning, preparedness, emergency-process and National Guard response failures and issued recommendations.

Boundary: Congressional oversight findings are not criminal judgments and do not adjudicate individual guilt.

View Official Source →
J6-E-0013GOVERNMENT FINDING

Planned in Plain Sight — Intelligence Failures Before January 6

Establishes: A later Senate investigation identified warnings received by FBI and DHS I&A and failures in processing, assessing or disseminating some threat information.

Boundary: It does not establish that every warning was credible or that every official possessed the same information.

View Official Source →
J6-E-0014ALLEGATION

Oath Keepers Seditious-Conspiracy Indictment

Establishes: A federal grand jury charged eleven defendants, including Elmer Stewart Rhodes III, with seditious conspiracy and other offenses.

Boundary: An indictment contains allegations, not findings of guilt. Later outcomes are recorded separately.

View Official Source →
J6-E-0015JURY VERDICT

Rhodes and Meggs Oath Keepers Seditious-Conspiracy Verdict

Establishes: A federal jury found Rhodes and Kelly Meggs guilty of seditious conspiracy and other offenses after trial.

Boundary: The verdict establishes outcomes for the defendants and counts adjudicated in that trial, not unrelated people.

View Official Source →
J6-E-0016JURY VERDICT

Four Additional Oath Keepers Seditious-Conspiracy Verdicts

Establishes: A federal jury found Roberto Minuta, Joseph Hackett, David Moerschel and Edward Vallejo guilty of seditious conspiracy and other charges.

Boundary: These verdicts concern the defendants and charges adjudicated in that trial.

View Official Source →
J6-E-0017ALLEGATION

Proud Boys Seditious-Conspiracy Superseding Indictment

Establishes: A federal grand jury charged five Proud Boys members with seditious conspiracy and other offenses.

Boundary: The indictment records allegations and the procedural fact that charges were filed; it does not itself establish guilt.

View Official Source →
J6-E-0018JURY VERDICT

Proud Boys Leaders Seditious-Conspiracy Verdicts

Establishes: A federal jury found Enrique Tarrio, Ethan Nordean, Joseph Biggs and Zachary Rehl guilty of seditious conspiracy; five defendants were convicted of multiple felonies.

Boundary: The verdicts do not establish that every Proud Boys member or January 6 participant shared the same conduct, intent or liability.

View Official Source →
J6-E-0019SENTENCING

Rhodes and Meggs Sentencing Record

Establishes: The court imposed sentences following their convictions, including an 18-year prison sentence for Rhodes.

Boundary: Later appeals or executive clemency may alter legal consequences but do not erase the historical sentencing event.

View Official Source →
J6-E-0020SENTENCING

Enrique Tarrio Sentencing Record

Establishes: Following his trial convictions, the court sentenced Tarrio to 22 years in prison.

Boundary: Later appellate or executive actions should be recorded separately without erasing the earlier verdict and sentence.

View Official Source →
