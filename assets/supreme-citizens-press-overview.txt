ABOUT THE INITIATIVE
The Supreme Citizens Charter is a civic and educational framework centered on constitutional citizenship, equal civic dignity, public stewardship, accountable government, and peaceful civic participation. It invites people across political, ideological, cultural, and regional differences to learn about their constitutional system and take an active role in civic life.

THE CENTRAL IDEA
We the People. Constitutional Citizenship. Public Stewardship.
The Supreme Citizens identity expresses the collective popular sovereignty of the people within the constitutional system. It calls citizens to learn, participate, question, serve, and help preserve the Republic. No individual is placed above the Constitution or the law.

EXPLORE THE WEBSITE
Visitors can read the Charter and its 12 Principles, explore the Supreme Citizens identity and Citizens Branch concept, and find practical participation resources. The site also provides links to Congress, voting information, founding documents, and official government updates.

THE 12 PRINCIPLES
1. Popular Sovereignty
2. Equal Citizenship
3. Constitutional Supremacy
4. Distributed Constitutional Power
5. Unitary but Limited Executive Authority
6. Temporary Public Stewardship
7. Authority Requires Accountability
8. Protected Liberty and Rule of Law
9. Checks, Balances, and Institutional Independence
10. Electoral and Civic Accountability
11. Constitution Before Party
12. Posterity

CIVIC & EDUCATIONAL FRAMEWORK
The Charter is a civic and educational document. It does not replace the U.S. Constitution, confer governmental authority, or provide legal advice. Citizens and public officials alike remain subject to the Constitution and the rule of law.
