Identifier Guide

Know what kind of record you are reading

J6-E · Evidence

Sources, official records and evidentiary classifications.

J6-C · Claims

Claims separated from the evidence used to assess them.

J6-T · Timeline

Events and chronology with stable identifiers.

J6-S · Security

Warnings, intelligence, requests, authorization and response.

J6-L · Legal

Proceedings, prosecutions, rulings, dispositions and clemency.

J6-V · Voices

Testimony, observation, interviews and conflicting accounts.

J6-A · Analysis

Structured citizen-analysis questions and evidence assessment.

Research rule: a cross-reference shows relevance, not proof. A date does not establish motive. A charge does not establish guilt. A congressional finding is not a judicial conviction. Clemency does not rewrite the historical procedural record. Testimony should be evaluated according to whether it is direct, secondhand, corroborated, disputed or later revised.

Recommended Research Path

Move from source to conclusion

Evidence → Timeline → Institutional Response → Legal Record → Testimony → Citizen Analysis → Record Maintenance

When later information changes the record, use Record Maintenance to understand what was verified, corrected, superseded or archived and why.
