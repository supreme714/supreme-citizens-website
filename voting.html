Voting & Voter Registration

Use official government resources to register to vote, check your voter registration, find state election information, review deadlines, and learn how voting works where you live.

Start with your state

Register, update, or check your registration

Voting rules and registration procedures are administered by the states and territories. The official Vote.gov service lets you select your state or territory and connects you to voter-registration and election resources.

Select My State on Vote.gov

What you can do

Depending on your state, official resources may let you register to vote, update your name or address, change party affiliation where applicable, check registration status, review deadlines, and find polling information.

State-by-state information

What to verify before an election

Election rules, deadlines, early-voting periods, absentee or mail-ballot procedures, identification requirements, and polling locations can vary by state.

Before voting, confirm the current rules through your state or local election office.

Recommended checks

1. Confirm that your registration is active.
2. Verify your current address.
3. Check your registration deadline.
4. Review early-voting or absentee-ballot options.
5. Confirm your polling location and voting hours.
6. Review any identification requirements.
