Claim Tracker

What the record supports — and what it does not

Method: Claims are separated from evidence and classified according to the procedural and evidentiary status of the cited record. Congressional findings, allegations, official records and judicial rulings are not interchangeable.

J6-C-0001

Official Electoral College result

SUPPORTED BY OFFICIAL RECORD

The official 2020 Electoral College result recorded 306 electoral votes for Joseph R. Biden Jr. and 232 for Donald J. Trump.

Evidence: J6-E-0001

Qualification: This describes the official certified result; it does not by itself adjudicate every allegation concerning election administration.

J6-C-0002

Congress considered and rejected the Arizona objection

SUPPORTED BY PRIMARY RECORD

Congress considered objections during the January 6–7 proceedings and rejected the Arizona objection.

Evidence: J6-E-0002 · J6-E-0003 · J6-E-0004

Qualification: The votes establish Congress's disposition of the objection; they are not judicial findings on every underlying allegation.

J6-C-0003

Pre-event threat information and later oversight findings

SUPPORTED BY GOVERNMENT FINDING

Federal agencies possessed warning information about potential threats before January 6, while later oversight identified shortcomings in processing or sharing some information.

Evidence: J6-E-0005 · J6-E-0006

Qualification: The record does not establish that every agency had identical information or predicted the precise events that occurred.

J6-C-0004

Select Committee findings and public archive

SUPPORTED BY CONGRESSIONAL RECORD

The House Select Committee issued findings and recommendations and released supporting materials for public review.

Evidence: J6-E-0007

Qualification: Committee conclusions and referrals are congressional investigative findings, not criminal convictions.

J6-C-0005

Senate security and response review

SUPPORTED BY GOVERNMENT FINDING

The bipartisan Senate review identified breakdowns in intelligence handling, security preparation, coordination and response surrounding January 6.

Evidence: J6-E-0008

Qualification: The Senate report is an oversight finding and does not adjudicate individual criminal guilt.

J6-C-0006

Federal prosecutions require case-specific descriptions

SUPPORTED / REQUIRES CASE-SPECIFIC OUTCOME

Federal January 6 cases included arrests, charges, pleas, trials, convictions, dismissals and sentences; a criminal charge alone is not a finding of guilt.

Evidence: J6-E-0009

Qualification: Individual defendants must be described according to their actual case disposition rather than grouped together as convicted.

J6-C-0007

Fischer narrowed one federal obstruction provision

SUPPORTED BY COURT FINDING / LATER DEVELOPMENT

The Supreme Court's Fischer decision narrowed the interpretation of 18 U.S.C. § 1512(c)(2), affecting a subset of January 6 prosecutions rather than all January 6 cases.

Evidence: J6-E-0010

Qualification: The decision concerns the statutory scope of § 1512(c)(2) and does not erase unrelated charges, convictions or conduct in other cases.
