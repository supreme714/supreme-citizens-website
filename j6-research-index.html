Cross-Reference Standard

Connection is not conclusion

Use the records together, but preserve their categories.

Evidence → Event → Institutional Response → Legal Status → Testimony → Citizen Analysis

A shared identifier can show that two records concern the same event or issue. Chronological sequence alone does not prove causation. A congressional finding is not a criminal conviction. A charge is not guilt. Clemency is not an appellate reversal. A witness account may establish direct experience while still requiring comparison with documents and other witnesses.
