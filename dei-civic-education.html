Three principles. One commitment.

Dignity

Respect the inherent worth of every person, including those with whom we disagree.

Equality

Protect each person's rights under the law and oppose unlawful discrimination.

Individuality

Recognize each person's freedom, character, qualifications, and responsibility.

This is a proposed Supreme Citizens civic interpretation. The established meaning of DEI is Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion. The Citizens Branch is a proposed civic participation framework, not a fourth constitutional branch.

The DEI Declaration

We stand for Dignity. We defend Equality. We celebrate Individuality.

Different People. Equal Rights. One Republic.

We affirm that every person possesses inherent worth, that government must respect constitutional and statutory rights, and that each individual deserves to be considered without unlawful discrimination or stereotypes.

Congress legislates, the President executes federal law, and the courts resolve cases and controversies. Citizens participate, question, petition, vote, and hold public officials accountable.

WE THE PEOPLE ARE NOT AN AUDIENCE.
