Federal inspection and warranty protections

FAR 52.246-12, Inspection of Construction, where incorporated, requires contractor inspection records and generally requires correction of nonconforming work without charge, subject to its terms. FAR 52.246-21, Warranty of Construction, when included, generally establishes a one-year warranty from acceptance. Neither clause should be asserted as applicable to this specific contract without examining its incorporated terms.

Records request and oversight checklist

Requested records for a proposed Freedom of Information Act submission: executed award and modifications; sole-source justification; price-reasonableness assessment; full restoration specifications; quality control records; government inspections; acceptance documentation; warranties; complaints and condition evaluations; invoices and actual payments; correspondence about streaking and any correction plans.

FOIA status: Proposed; not submitted by Supreme Citizens as part of this review. To submit a request, use FOIA.gov and select the Department of the Interior or National Park Service.
