How we evaluate public spending

  1. Identify and link the original government appropriation, contract, budget or official action.
  2. Distinguish requested funding, enacted appropriations, obligations and actual payments.
  3. Identify competition method, contract modifications, inspection, acceptance and warranty terms.
  4. Compare official agency explanations with independent reports and available technical evidence.
  5. Track outstanding records requests, disclosures and corrective action without presuming wrongdoing.
  6. Date every review and revise findings when reliable new documentation appears.
Evidence and neutrality: A pending allegation is not proof of waste, fraud or defective performance. These public research pages identify documentary sources, unresolved questions and the limits of available evidence. This is a nonpartisan civic-education resource.
